06. Campaign Execution System

How each campaign should be built, segmented, measured, and reused

Google Search campaign segmentation

  • Separate Brand, High-Intent Non-Brand, Platform Search, Feature Search, Price Intent, and Competitor Alternative campaigns.
  • Use exact and phrase match first, then test broad match only after conversion tracking and negative keyword patterns are reliable.
  • Build ad groups around one decision context: PS5 compatibility, Xbox compatibility, wireless with mic, headset for glasses, streaming headset, premium headset, and under-$200 intent.
  • Use keyword-level landing page mapping so each ad group sends users to the most relevant page, not a generic collection page.
  • Review search terms daily during the launch period and weekly after stabilization.

Shopping and Merchant Center approach

  • Optimize product titles with product type, platform compatibility, wireless or wired, mic, color, and primary differentiator.
  • Audit Merchant Center for disapprovals, GTINs, availability, pricing consistency, image quality, shipping, tax, and return settings.
  • Segment product groups by wireless, wired, premium price tier, platform compatibility, best sellers, and margin priority.
  • Use custom labels for margin level, price tier, hero products, seasonal products, and promotion status.
  • Exclude weak products from scaling until product-level ROAS and conversion rate are validated.

Meta campaign approach

  • Launch Advantage+ Shopping for catalog-based sales once the pixel and purchase events are verified.
  • Test creative angles by use case: FPS advantage, clear mic, comfort with glasses, console compatibility, setup upgrade, and gift purchase.
  • Build retargeting audiences for product viewers, add-to-cart users, checkout starters, video viewers, and engaged social users.
  • Use lookalikes after enough purchase and high-quality add-to-cart data is collected.
  • Separate prospecting creative tests from remarketing offers so results are not misread.

Data collection and audience reuse

  • Track view item, add to cart, begin checkout, purchase, revenue, product ID, device, landing page, campaign, and audience source.
  • Create remarketing lists by intent: PS5 viewers, Xbox viewers, wireless viewers, premium viewers, cart abandoners, checkout abandoners, and repeat buyers.
  • Use purchase data to build best-customer audiences and identify high-AOV product combinations.
  • Feed winning search terms into SEO content, landing pages, Shopping titles, Meta creative hooks, and FAQ blocks.
  • Use CRM, email, and SMS flows for cart recovery, product education, post-purchase cross-sell, and winback.

09. Client feedback handling

Client Feedback Response Framework

Feedback A: ROAS is dropping

Diagnosis should separate media inflation, tracking issues, conversion decline, product mix changes, and competitor pressure.

CPC increased, conversion rate dropped, AOV fell, tracking broke, weaker campaign got more budget, competitors launched discounts, bad search terms entered, mobile checkout worsened, or product feed quality declined.
Check conversion tracking, revenue tracking, search terms, campaign ROAS, product-level ROAS, device performance, landing page CVR, AOV trend, cart abandonment rate, product feed status, and competitor offers.
Pause low-ROAS segments, add negatives, move budget to winners, improve feed titles, push best sellers, test stronger offers, improve landing pages, recover cart abandoners, and use tROAS only after enough conversion volume exists.

Feedback B: Traffic but sales are low

This is a funnel and traffic-quality problem. The fix is not simply “more budget.”

Wrong keyword intent, poor landing page match, weak reviews, unclear shipping, unclear returns, weak warranty proof, high price friction, slow mobile page, checkout friction, no express payment, or weak cart recovery.
Review search terms, exclude low-intent traffic, match ad groups to specific pages, add comparison tables, show reviews near CTA, show shipping cost early, clarify returns, add trust badges, and simplify mobile checkout.
Launch cart abandonment remarketing, abandoned cart email, limited-time offer test, bundle test, free-shipping threshold, product-specific landing pages, express checkout, and mobile page-speed cleanup.

Final recommendation

Recommended Strategic Direction

Sampleton should be positioned around cross-platform compatibility, clear voice communication, long-session comfort, and mid-premium value. The acquisition system should connect high-intent search, product-feed quality, relevant landing pages, reliable tracking, and cart recovery.

1
Build platform-specific relevance.Create dedicated landing pages for PS5, Xbox, PC, Switch, Mac, and VR and align ad copy with each page.
2
Strengthen product proof.Use reviews, warranty information, return policy clarity, shipping details, comfort claims, and product comparison modules.
3
Launch with budget discipline.Prioritize exact and phrase match Search, clean Shopping feed coverage, verified tracking, and controlled remarketing before scaling.
4
Report with action-oriented insight.Separate business KPIs from diagnostic signals and connect each finding to a clear optimization action.
